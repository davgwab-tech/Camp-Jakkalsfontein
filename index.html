Frequently Asked QuestionsGereelde Vrae

What are the check-in and check-out times? Hoe laat is inklok en uitklok?
Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is strictly at 10:00 AM. Inklok is vanaf 14:00 en uitklok is stiptelik om 10:00.
Is firewood available on the farm? Is kaggelhout beskikbaar op die plaas?
Yes, we sell firewood as an add-on for R100. You can select this option in the booking form. Ja, ons verkoop kaggelhout as 'n byvoeging vir R100. Jy kan dit op die besprekingsvorm kies.
Are the swimming pools completely private? Is die swembaddens heeltemal privaat?
Swimming pools are shared facilities. Exclusive use can sometimes be arranged (subject to availability and fees). Swembaddens is gedeelde geriewe. Eksklusiewe gebruik kan soms gereël word (onderhewig aan beskikbaarheid).
What are the rules and capacity for booking the Entire Farm? Wat is die reëls en kapasiteit vir die bespreking van die Hele Plaas?
Entire Farm bookings have a maximum limit of 30 people. If your group exceeds 30 people, please contact us directly. Hele Plaas besprekings het 'n maksimum limiet van 30 mense. Indien jou groep 30 mense oorskry, kontak ons asseblief direk.
What are the details for booking the Entire Farm & Campsite? Wat is die besonderhede vir die bespreking van die Hele Plaas & Kampterrein?
The Entire Farm & Campsite package accommodates a maximum of 250 people. Pricing for this exclusive booking is to be discussed upon inquiry. Die Hele Plaas & Kampterrein pakket akkommodeer 'n maksimum van 250 mense. Pryse vir hierdie eksklusiewe bespreking moet bespreek word.
